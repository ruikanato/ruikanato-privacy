Privacy Policy – Rui Kanato Threads Tools

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Threads API app (“Rui Kanato Threads Tools”) is operated by Rui Kanato, an independent author, and is used only with the author’s own Threads account, @ruikanato.author. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

Data we access

Through the official Threads API, the app:

  • publishes posts written and approved by the author to the author’s own account;
  • reads insights (such as views, likes, replies, profile views, follower count and link clicks) for the author’s own account and posts;
  • searches recent public Threads posts by keyword, to find readers discussing romance books.

Data we store

  • The author’s own account: post IDs, publishing logs and insight numbers, kept in local records on the author’s computer.
  • Public posts found by search: only the post ID, permalink, username, timestamp and the first 200 characters of the text, together with the author’s private notes (a relevance score and an optional reply draft). These records are deleted automatically once they are 30 days old (checked every day).
  • Access tokens: stored only in the Windows Credential Manager on the author’s computer. They are never written to files, logs or this website.

How we use data

Search results are used only so the author can read relevant reader conversations and decide personally whether to reply in the Threads app. The app never replies, likes, follows or sends messages automatically. We do not sell or share data, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data to build profiles of individuals.

Data deletion

See the Data Deletion Instructions. In short: email ruikanato.author@gmail.com with your Threads username, and any stored records about your public posts will be deleted within 30 days.
