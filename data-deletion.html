Data Deletion Instructions – Rui Kanato Threads Tools

Last updated: October 30, 2026

If one of your public Threads posts appeared in our search

  1. Email ruikanato.author@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include your Threads username. A link to the post is helpful but not required.
  3. We will delete every stored record about your posts within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Even without a request, records about public posts found by search are deleted automatically once they are 30 days old (checked every day).

If you are a user of this app

The app is used only by the author’s own account. To remove its access to a Threads account, open Threads or Instagram settings, go to the website permissions / apps and websites section, and remove the Rui Kanato app listed there. You can also email the address above to have all related records deleted.
